    How to play

    • Expand: click a neutral tile touching your land. Each tile costs 5 troops; the claim floods outward until the committed force runs out.
    • Attack: click any tile of a bordering enemy. Your force fights along the whole shared border, a few tiles per tick, so the defender keeps regenerating while you push. A tile's defense is the defender's troops divided by its border tiles. Taking it costs you 2.5× that plus 5. If your force can't beat a tile, the attack ends and the rest is lost.
    • Troops grow with your land, slowing as your pool fills. Cap is 100 per tile plus 2000 per City. Recruit turns 500 gold into 1000 troops.
    • Gold comes only from trains. A Factory lays track to up to 4 of your (or your allies') buildings within 30 tiles, routed through land you hold. Every 4 s each line sends a train; each delivery pays 30 + 3 per tile of track, ×1.5 into a City. Allies earn half when your trains reach their buildings. Losing land under a track cuts the line.
    • Buildings take 3×3 tiles of your own land. City: +2000 troop cap, ×1.5 defense on its tiles. Defense Post: ×2 defense on your land within 6 tiles (the hatched zone). Missile Silo: lets you launch nukes. Buildings change hands when their center tile is captured.
    • Nukes: wipe a radius of 5, destroy buildings, kill troops of whoever owned the land, and leave fallout for 30 s. Silos reload for 30 s. Nuking an ally breaks the alliance.
    • Alliances: propose from the Factions panel. Allies cannot attack each other. Breaking an alliance marks you as a traitor for 60 s: everyone prefers to attack you and nobody will ally with you.
    • Win: hold 60% of the land, be the last faction standing, or keep every survivor allied with you for 60 s once the map is full.
    • Controls: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, 0 resets the view. Space pauses, 1–6 pick a tool, Shift-click keeps building, Esc or right-click cancels, F toggles fullscreen.
